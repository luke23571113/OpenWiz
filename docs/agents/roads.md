# Jake's three roads

Jake gives one yes on every road. Nothing ships without it.

| Road | What Jake uses it for | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chat for small work | A focused change he can explain and approve in a conversation. | Fix a typo in a settings label. |
| Grill, then spec, then plan graph for medium work | A feature that needs design decisions, a written outcome, and a plan for what to build first. | Add report export with agreed formats and separate tickets for the export and its interface. |
| Wayfind for very big work | An effort that spans several specs or versions. Jake maps the decisions and works through them in order. | Plan a new product from its first prototype through several releases. |
